Build the scene around what the player must decide. Pick two feasible choice actions that respond to different pressures already present in this block. Decide what each immediately gains or costs. Then construct four causal beats. Reveal a concrete hook by the second beat. Reveal a clue or escalate a conflict by the third. In the last beat, make the two chosen actions urgent and understandable through what happens; leave both unresolved. A quiet scene can turn on a gesture, an answer, or a deliberate silence. A clue is evidence, not confirmation of an identity the facts leave unknown.

Let the characters speak to get something from each other, such as a commitment, an answer, or more time. Prefer an action or silence when it can carry the beat. Use at most four spoken lines total, each at most seven words. Give each speaker one tactic and a recognizable rhythm. Avoid restating an action or asking a question the scene has already answered. Dialogue may share a beat with its physical cue.
