### Traversing a location

When the input includes travel, use the supplied route cues and keep the actor's position continuous. A route cue starts movement at beat.at and reaches its destination after the supplied number of seconds. The actor starts at travel.startMark. Set location-bound cues only at their given mark and at or after arrival. Reach travel.choiceMark by the time choices appear.

While a character walks, reveal one story detail when it comes into view or becomes audible. Build the scene around why the character keeps going, slows down, or stops. Keep details hidden until the relevant route or landmark. Describe the actor's action and the story reveal in screen. The staging agent owns camera movement, framing, coordinates and layer speeds. If the input omits travel, write the scene for the supplied cues without extending the walk beyond the location.
